    How a lateral flow immunoassay works

    • Sample pad takes up the liquid sample.
    • Conjugate pad holds dried antibodies tagged with red gold nanoparticles. Any target in the sample binds to them here.
    • Nitrocellulose membrane (the stationary phase) carries liquid by capillary action past two lines of immobilized antibody:
      • T line: capture antibodies grab the target, which is already bound to a gold conjugate, forming a "sandwich." Gold collects there and a red line appears.
      • C line: anti-IgG antibodies grab the gold conjugate whether or not target is attached. This shows the liquid flowed and the reagents work.
    • Absorbent pad acts as a wick, pulling liquid through and soaking up the excess.

    Reading the result

    Window showsInterpretation
    C   TPositive (target detected)
    C onlyNegative (no target above the detection limit)
    No C line (with or without T)Invalid: repeat with a new test

    Even a faint T line counts as positive. A real sample with a low load but good flow often shows a faint T and a strong C. Person C models a failed run: too little sample, so the liquid front stalls before reaching either line.
